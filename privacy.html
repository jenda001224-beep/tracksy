2. The short version

Tracksy has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking code of any kind. We operate no servers. Everything Tracksy remembers about you — your swipes, likes, collections and settings — is stored only on your own device and is never uploaded to us.

3. What is stored on your device

The following is written to your device's local storage and never leaves it, except where section 4 says otherwise:

  • Your swipe history (tracks you liked, skipped or marked as fire)
  • Your saved collections
  • Recent searches and the genres you picked during onboarding
  • Usage counters: sessions, total swipes, daily swipe count, daily streak
  • Your settings: language, appearance, offline mode and its storage limit, reminder preferences
  • Offline cache: cover artwork and 30-second audio previews you chose to download

You can erase this at any time from within the app (clear history, delete collections, delete offline downloads) or by deleting the app.

4. What leaves your device

Search requests to Apple. To find music, Tracksy sends your search terms — including song or artist names you type, genres you select, and text extracted from a screenshot you upload — to Apple's public iTunes Search API. As with any internet request, Apple also receives your IP address and technical request data. We receive no copy of this. Apple's handling is governed by Apple's own privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Cover art and audio previews are downloaded from Apple's content servers.

Apple Music playlist. If you tap "add to playlist", Tracksy asks iOS for permission to your music library and adds that track to a playlist named "Tracksy" in your Apple Music account. Only the track's catalogue ID is handed to Apple's MediaPlayer framework on your device. We never see your library and we receive nothing back. You can withdraw this permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Media & Apple Music.

Nothing else. We send no data to ourselves or to any other company.

5. Screenshots and text recognition

If you upload a screenshot, the text is recognised entirely on your device using Apple's on-device Vision framework. The image is never uploaded anywhere and is not retained after you leave the screen. Only the recognised song titles are then used as search terms as described in section 4.

6. Purchases

Subscriptions and one-off purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your name, payment card, or billing address. The app only receives a yes/no signal from Apple's StoreKit confirming whether your purchase is active.

7. Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. There is no push server and no notification content leaves the device.

8. Sharing to Instagram

If you tap share, Tracksy renders an image on your device and places it on the system pasteboard so Instagram can pick it up, then opens Instagram. This only happens when you actively choose to share. What Instagram does afterwards is governed by Instagram's own privacy policy.

9. Legal basis for processing (GDPR Article 6)

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — providing the discovery features you asked for, including sending your search terms to Apple, and delivering purchased features.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — keeping local counters and history so the app can personalise your feed and avoid showing you the same track twice. You can clear this data at any time.

We do not rely on consent, we do not profile you for advertising, and we make no automated decisions producing legal effects.

Music preferences could in unusual cases hint at beliefs or opinions that GDPR Article 9 treats as sensitive. We never analyse your data for such purposes, and because the data stays on your device, we have no access to it.

10. Retention

Data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Because we hold no copy, deletion on your device is final.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, and objection, and the right to withdraw consent where processing relies on it.

In practice: because all of your data is on your own device and we hold none of it, you exercise these rights directly in the app — view your history, delete individual items, clear everything, or uninstall. If you nevertheless want to contact us about your rights, write to the email in section 1.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In the Czech Republic this is the Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů), www.uoou.cz.

12. International transfers

Search requests go to Apple, which operates infrastructure worldwide including the United States. Apple participates in the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and offers standard contractual clauses. We ourselves transfer nothing.

13. Children

Tracksy is not directed at children under 13. The music catalogue is Apple's and may include tracks marked explicit. Parents can restrict explicit content and purchases using iOS Screen Time.

14. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will ship with the app and the effective date above will change.
